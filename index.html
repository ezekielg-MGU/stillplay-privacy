Stillplay privacy policy

Last updated October 15, 2026

Stillplay is a browser extension that hides distracting parts of YouTube. It does not collect, store on any server, sell or share any personal data.

What Stillplay stores

Only your own settings: which options are on or off, and the channels you chose to block. They're kept in your browser's extension storage (chrome.storage.sync). If Chrome Sync is turned on in your browser, Chrome syncs them to your other computers through your Google account, the same way it syncs your bookmarks. Stillplay itself never sees or receives them.

What Stillplay doesn't do

  • It makes no network requests and has no server.
  • It has no analytics, tracking, ads or remote code.
  • It doesn't read, record or send your browsing or watch history.

Where it runs

Only on youtube.com and m.youtube.com, where it changes how pages look. When you use the Watch history × button, it presses YouTube's own "Remove from watch history" option for you; that request goes from your browser to YouTube, exactly as if you'd clicked it yourself.

Removing your data

Uninstalling Stillplay removes its settings from your browser. You can also export them first from the side panel.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
